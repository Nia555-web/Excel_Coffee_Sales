<h1>Coffee Sales Analysis – Excel</h1>
<h3>About the Project</h3>
<p>After completing my Data Technician Bootcamp, I wanted to continue practising and developing the skills I had learned, particularly in Excel and data analysis.

For this project, I analysed a coffee sales dataset from Kaggle. I chose a coffee shop dataset because I have previous experience managing coffee shops, so I was interested in exploring the data from both an analytical and business perspective.

The aim of the project was to practise data cleaning, data transformation, Excel formulas, PivotTables and data visualisation, while looking for patterns and trends in coffee sales.</p>

<h2>Summary or my findings</h2>
<li>Latte and Americano with milk generated the highest sales.</li>
<li>Although Americano with milk had more cups sold, Latte generated higher revenue, showing that sales volume and revenue can tell different stories.</li>
<li>Cash payments were more common than card payments in this dataset.</li>
<li>October and February had the highest monthly sales, while January had the lowest, suggesting some seasonal variation.</li>
<li>Fourth bar chart demonstrates the sales throughout the week, showing that weekends tend to be less busy than the weekdays.</li>
<li>Last chart demonstrates sales variations throughout the day, with different coffee types showing different levels of popularity across time periods.</li>
